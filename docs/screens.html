Catwalk · Screen design

Every screen, and what is on it

One rule for the whole app: each screen answers one question. If you cannot say what question a screen answers, it is two screens.

The map of the app

Five tabs at most. Everything you do every day is on the first tab. Everything else is one tap away, never two.

Tab Answers When
Home How far am I today, and how is my cat? Now becomes the room in slice 3
Map Where can I walk to? Slice 4
Cats Who do I have, and who is next? Now one cat (the Cat tab today); a collection in slice 5
Friends Who is walking, and who needs a nudge? Slice 6
Me How have I been doing, and my settings Now History today; Settings joins it
Today the app has Home, Cat, History. The change is small: History becomes Me and absorbs Settings (no more gear icon), the Cat tab becomes Cats. Map and Friends tabs only appear when their slice ships, so nobody sees an empty tab.

NowOnboarding

Question: who is my cat, and what am I promising it?

  • 1 Welcome: the cat, the promise in one line, one button.
  • 2 Pick a coat: three real cats, not three colours.
  • 3 Name it: keyboard opens at once. Suggest a few names.
  • 4 Goal: a slider instead of a typed number. Shows what the goal pays and how many cats it lets you keep. This is where the goal-to-reward link is taught.
  • 5 Step access: explain why, then ask. Denying never blocks finishing.

Do not: add an account or sign-up. Let people meet the cat first.

NowHome

Question: how far am I today, and is my cat happy?

  • The cat is the hero, drawn large, with the progress ring around her. The ring and the cat are one picture.
  • The step number is the biggest text on screen. The goal sits under it, small.
  • Streak gets a visible chip. It is the habit driver and it is currently hidden.
  • Level and XP shrink to one quiet line. Detail lives on the Cats screen.
  • The cat's mood is shown by the cat (posture, eyes) and one word, never colour alone.
  • One action at most below the number, and only when needed: Allow step access, or Find Mimi.
  • Steps refresh every 30 seconds and when you return to the app. The ring animates between values.

Now one cat, slice 5 collection

Question: who do I have, and who is next?

  • Today: the one cat, big. Name (tap to rename), coat, mood and status, level, XP bar and "XP to next level", missed days.
  • Slice 5: a grid of every cat. Tap one to see her. Locked slots show the daily goal needed to keep another cat.
  • Each cat shows how she was found: spawned on the map, or photographed (3 of 5 sightings).
  • "Let go" is hidden inside a cat's menu, with a confirm. It is the only way to free a slot without raising the goal.
  • Toys owned are listed here. They appear in the room.

NowMe (History + Settings)

Question: how have I been doing?

  • Header: current streak and best streak, large.
  • This week strip: seven days at a glance. Teal = met, grey = missed, today outlined.
  • Day list, newest first: date, steps over goal, XP earned, met or not. Never colour alone (tick or cross too).
  • Totals: total steps, total XP, days met. A weekly average.
  • Settings sit behind the gear on this tab, not on Home.
  • Empty state for a new user: a friendly picture and "Finish today's goal and it shows up here."

Settings contents:

  • Daily goal (applies from tomorrow, say so)
  • Step access: status and a button to fix it
  • Notifications: which ones, and quiet hours
  • Sound and haptics
  • Account (slice 6 only)
  • Reset Catwalk, last, in red, with a confirm

Slice 4Runaway and coming home

Question: what do I have to do to get her back?

  • Home turns into a two-step checklist. The room is empty. The state is honest and a little sad, never scolding.
  • Step 1: a long walk, with its own progress bar.
  • Step 2: she appears as a pin on the map. Walk to it.
  • Both are required. A few steps do not bring her back.
  • Until the map exists (now), any steps bring her back and the screen is just the message.
  • When she returns: the quiet "comes home" moment, then normal Home.

Slice 4–5Map

Question: where can I walk to?

  • A calm map with you, nearby candy, and (later) a cat pin. Mapbox is the candidate.
  • Tap a pin: a bottom sheet with distance and what it might give.
  • It is for going somewhere. It is not a second step counter, and today's step total is not shown big here.
  • Reaching a pin shows the reward: extra XP, a streak freeze, a toy, or progress toward a cat.
  • Slice 5: a camera button for street cats. Photograph the same cat several times and she may join you.
  • Ask for location only here, at the moment it is needed, with a one-line reason.
  • No location: show a clear message, never an empty grey map.

Slice 6Friends

Question: who is walking, and who needs a nudge?

  • Today's board: friends ranked by steps today, with met / not yet.
  • Nudge on someone who has not met their goal. Limited to one per person per day.
  • Visit a room: see a friend's cat and toys. Read only.
  • Weekly leaderboard and your own statistics.
  • Add by invite link or code. No public search.
  • This is the first screen that needs an account. Before it, the app works with the phone alone.

Notifications

Many, but each one is one of these, and each is switchable. A little guilt is the product. A spam channel is not.

Notification When Example
Goal still open Evening, if not met "Mimi is waiting. 2,280 steps to go."
Streak at risk Late evening, streak ≥ 3 "Your 5 day streak ends tonight."
Cat about to run After the 2nd missed day "One more day and Mimi leaves."
Friend nudged you When it happens "Sam says: get walking."

Things that apply to every screen

  • Palette: orange (#F69337) for fills, the ring and the cat. Teal (#1F7A7A) for accents and "done". Dark ink for all text. Words never sit in orange on cream.
  • Thumb zone: everything you tap often is in the lower half. The top is for reading.
  • One main thing: at most one filled button per screen.
  • Text size: scales with the phone setting. Touch targets at least 48 px.
  • Honest numbers: if the phone has not told us, we say so. We never show a guess.
  • Mood is never colour alone: posture, an icon, and a word.
  • Dark Mode later, as deep teal-black with the same orange.

Decisions for you

  1. Does Home become the room? I'd say yes: the cat lives where you open the app. It saves a tab.
  2. Tab names: Home, Cats, Me. Or Home, Cat, Walks. "Me" is shortest.
  3. Language: English only for now, or Dutch too? Your logo's tagline is Dutch.
  4. Slider or typed number for the goal. I'd use a slider with ±500 nudge buttons.
  5. The cat's voice: does she speak? One warm line per state, written as the cat, or neutral text.

What to build first

  1. The cat art (a flat silhouette in your logo's style, three coats, three moods). Everything above depends on it.
  2. Home redesign: ring around the cat, big number, streak chip.
  3. Celebrations: goal reached and level up.
  4. Me tab: week strip, totals, settings moved inside.
  5. Slice 3, the room, then the map slice.
