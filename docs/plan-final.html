Catwalk: the final plan

Rules, flowcharts, build order and decisions on one page. Updated 2026-10-26 for candy and cat beds.

Walk enough each day to keep your cat. Reach the step goal and the cat is happy. Miss it and the cat gets sadder, then runs away. Getting it back takes a lot of walking in time, and then finding it on the map. Walking also earns candy, and candy buys cat beds, and each bed brings a new cat.

Changed in this version. XP and level are gone. Candy replaces them: you earn it by walking and spend it on toys and cat beds. Each bed costs more than the last, so that is the cap on cats. A new cat appears on the map the next morning and stays until you fetch it (D5, D8, D17 to D21). The numbers (candy amounts, bed prices, prizes) are mine, to tune after your phone test. See Decisions.

1. The daily loop

Walk enough each day to keep your cat. Half a goal is not the goal.

  • Today does not count as missed until it ends.
  • A freeze saves the day but gives no streak credit. At most 2 can be held.
  • Halfway pays nothing. The cat only reacts. It does not change whether the goal was reached.

2. Getting a runaway cat back

A deadline now. Walk enough in time and the cat appears on the map. Miss it and the cat leaves the room for good, but one day it may turn up on the map again.

  • Steps from before the cat ran away do not count.
  • Once the cat is on the map it stays until you find it. There is no second timer. The same goes for a lost cat that comes back.
  • A streak freeze never rescues a cat that has already run away.
  • A lost cat that comes back needs an empty bed. Its own bed stays reserved while it is lost, so there is always room.

3. Today's steps

Pays once a day. Opening the app again cannot pay it twice. The side quest is gone.

  • Candy follows the goal: 1 candy per 1,000 steps of your goal, at least 3 and at most 15 a day. 6 at 6,000. A higher goal pays more a day, not more per step.
  • There is no XP and no level. The numbers you see are steps today, your streak, total steps and candy.

4. First week

Most people quit in the first days, so the first week is easy and pays early.

  • Built. The lower-goal offer after a bad week is not built yet.
  • After 5 of 7 days met the app may offer +500. It is always an offer.

5. Every day: reasons to open the app

Small reasons, none of them a second score. Only the fresh pins exist today.

  • Reminders use the cat's voice, for example "Jip is waiting by the door". Settings has an off switch.
  • Friends-only leaderboards and a shared weekly goal come with the account, not before.

6. The map

Pickups to walk to. The map is for going somewhere, not a second step counter.

  • Up to 5 pickups a day, 250 to 900 m away, collected within 30 m.
  • Scratching posts are drawn and unlocked like toys. They do not exist yet.

7. Candy, beds and new cats

Walk, earn candy, buy a cat bed. A cat then turns up on the map and stays until you fetch it. Each bed costs more than the last, which is the cap.

  • A street cat you photograph and a lost cat that returns also need an empty bed. The first free bed takes the first cat that arrives.
  • A cat that runs away leaves its bed empty. The stray kitten, or the lost cat coming back, fills it.
  • If you travel far from home, a waiting cat moves to a new spot near you the next morning.
  • Toys cost 8 candy each. Decorations and treats are not planned for now.

8. The order, and the people part

Each slice is playable on its own. Everything before the last one works with the phone off the network.

  • Friends, visiting rooms and a leaderboard need an account. The walking, the cat, the map and the room live on the phone first.
  • Each notification is one of a few kinds, with no spam channel. At most 3 a day.

What I would not add

  • Money stakes. They put pressure on the wrong people and hurt trust.
  • A second scoreboard or a second goal. Steps stay the only one.
  • More daily tasks. Optional quests are switched off for now.
  • A global leaderboard as the fix for retention.

Rules that do not move

  • The phone's step record decides the day. The app never invents a step count.
  • Goal candy is paid once per calendar day. Today is in progress until it ends.
  • Widgets show state; the day evaluation decides it.
  • The local save is the source of truth. An account later only keeps a copy.

What I found

  • Your later ideas replace three earlier lines. New cat by level or XP became new cat from a bed. The cap by goal became a rising bed price. "No currency for now" became candy (D5, D8, D18).
  • One tension to watch. A higher goal earns more candy, so it unlocks cats sooner. That also makes a bigger goal easier to miss, and a miss costs the cat. This is a real trade, not a bug, but test it before you trust the numbers.
  • Not in your list but in the plan: the room's scratching posts have no art or unlock yet, and nothing has run on a real phone.
