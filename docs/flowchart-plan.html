The core loop: walk, or the cat leaves

You set a step goal. Meet it and the cat is happy. Miss it for long enough and the cat runs. Getting it back takes a real walk, then finding it on the map.

  • Getting the cat back needs both: a long walk, then walking to the cat on the map.
  • A higher goal pays more XP a day and, after 6,000, lets you keep more cats. XP stays proportional, so no goal is a better deal per step.
  • No currency until finding things is already fun.

The map, new cats and the room

Candy on the map. Walk to it. The map is for going somewhere, not a second step counter.

  • Lowering the goal never deletes a cat. It only blocks new ones.
  • Buying stays out. If a shop is added later, it spends something earned by walking.

The order, and the people part

Each slice is playable on its own. Everything before the last one works with the phone off the network.

  • Friends, visiting rooms and a leaderboard need an account. The walking, the cat, the map and the room live on the phone first.
  • Many notifications on purpose: a little guilt gets you walking. Each one is one of the four kinds, with no spam channel.
