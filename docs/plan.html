Catwalk

Walk, or the cat leaves.

You set a step goal. Meet it and the cat is happy. Miss it for too long and the cat runs. Getting it back takes a real walk, then finding it on the map.

The phone already counts the steps. Catwalk reads that record. It does not run its own pedometer, and it does not invent numbers.

The day

  • A daily goal, chosen at the start. Default 6,000, between 1,000 and 30,000.
  • A progress bar for today’s steps.
  • Hit the goal: the cat is happy, and you get XP once that day.
  • A higher goal pays more. More XP, better rewards, and more cat slots.
  • A streak grows while the goal is met. Keeping it earns a reward. A streak freeze, found on a walk, can save one missed day.
  • Miss a full day and the cat is sad. Two days, neglected. Three, it runs away. Today does not count until it ends.

The map

  • Candy on the map. Walk to it.
  • A pickup can be extra XP, a streak freeze, a toy, progress toward a new cat, or something to save.
  • After enough level or XP, a new cat appears somewhere on the map. Walk there to unlock it.
  • Street cats can be photographed. The same cat, seen enough times, may unlock.
  • The map is for going somewhere. It is not a second step counter.
Open

Whether toys and cats are only found, or can also be bought. No currency until that is chosen.

Coming home

  • A runaway cat does not return because you took a few steps.
  • First a long walk. Then the cat is somewhere on the map. Walk to it.
  • Both are required.

The room

  • One room where every unlocked cat walks around.
  • Toys, scratching posts, and the rest appear there once you have them.
  • How many cats you can keep depends on your daily goal. A small goal means fewer cats.

Other people

  • Add friends.
  • Visit each other’s cat room.
  • Nudge a friend who has not hit their goal.
  • Statistics, and a leaderboard.
  • A lot of notifications. The point is a little guilt, so you walk.

What this needs

Friends, rooms you can visit, and a leaderboard need an account. The walking, the cat, the map pickups, and the room can exist on the phone first. The account syncs that. It does not decide whether the cat is happy.

Already on the phone: onboarding, one cat, today’s goal, XP, mood, and a local save. The map, the room, extra cats, photos, streaks, friends, and the leaderboard are the rest of the same app.

Each slice is playable on its own. Stop after one and check it before the next.

Order

  1. Finish the day on the phone.
  2. Read real steps.
  3. Add the room, with one cat.
  4. Add the map, still without other people.
  5. Add photos, extra cats, and the goal cap.
  6. Add an account, friends, and the leaderboard.

The map does not come first. A candy you cannot walk to is a picture. Real steps come before the map so the walk is the phone’s walk, not a fake one.

1. The day Next

Onboarding, one cat, today’s bar, XP, and mood already exist. This slice finishes that loop.

  • History of past days: steps, goal, met or not, XP.
  • Streak. A missed day breaks it. Today does not break it.
  • Goal size changes the daily XP. A higher goal pays more. The exact curve is a number we can tune, not a new system.
  • Cat screen: coat, level, mood, status. Settings: change the goal, reset.
  • Reset clears the local save and returns to onboarding.
  • Coming home stays “any steps” until the map exists. Do not build a fake map recovery.

2. Real steps After 1

Replace the debug buttons. Do not add a second counter.

  • iPhone reads Apple Health. Android reads Health Connect.
  • If that is denied, read the phone step sensor.
  • If neither exists, say steps are unavailable. Show zero only when the phone says zero.
  • A short background wake can sync the record. It does not count steps.
  • Mock buttons stay in debug builds.

3. The room After 2

One room, the cat you already have. This is where the cat lives when you are not walking. Toys come with the map.

  • The cat acts on her own: walking, sitting, sleeping, grooming, climbing and perching. Mood colours the odds; it never overrides the day's rules.
  • The room is built for a list of cats, and renders one today. Unlocking a second cat stays in slice 5.
  • Mood is visible here too. The room does not decide it.
  • No shop and no currency.

4. The map After 3

Places to walk to. Still one cat. Still no account.

  • A map of where you are. Mapbox is the candidate. Pick it when this slice starts, not before.
  • Candy near the player. Walk to it. The phone’s step record still decides the day. The map only checks that you reached the pin.
  • A pickup is one of: extra XP, a streak freeze, a toy for the room, or progress toward a cat. One table of rewards, not five systems.
  • A streak freeze saves one missed day, then it is gone.
  • A toy, once found, appears in the room.
  • Runaway recovery changes here (built). A long walk first, then the cat is placed on the map. Both are required. Until this slice, any steps still bring it home.

5. More cats After 4

  • After enough XP, a new cat spawns on the map. Walk there to unlock it.
  • Photograph a street cat. The same cat, enough times, can unlock. The camera is only for that.
  • Unlocked cats walk in the room.
  • The daily goal caps how many you can keep. Lowering the goal does not delete a cat. It blocks new ones until you raise it or let one go.
  • Buying stays out. If a shop is added later, it spends something earned by walking. It does not replace finding.

6. Other people Last

This is the first slice that needs an account. Everything before it works with the phone off the network.

  • Account. The phone remains the source of the cat’s mood and XP. The server stores a copy.
  • Add friends. Visit their room. Nudge someone who missed the goal.
  • Your own statistics, then a leaderboard of friends.
  • Notifications: goal still open, streak at risk, cat about to run, a friend nudged you. Many, but each one is one of those. No spam channel.

Rules that do not move

  • Widgets show state. evaluateDay decides it.
  • XP for the daily goal is awarded once per calendar day.
  • Today is in progress until it ends.
  • Production never invents a step count.
  • No currency until finding things is already fun.

Left open on purpose

  • The exact XP bonus for a higher goal.
  • How many steps count as the long walk home.
  • How many photos unlock a street cat.
  • The goal-to-cat-slot table.
  • Whether a shop exists at all.

Those are numbers. They can wait until the slice that needs them.

iOS and Android. Steps come from Apple Health or Health Connect, then the phone sensor.
